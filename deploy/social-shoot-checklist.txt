Social shoot checklist — 20 min on site
========================================
Use on every active job. One shoot feeds Mon/Wed/Fri Reels + Stories + Sat WhatsApp.

Before you leave office
-----------------------
[ ] Phone charged (>50%)
[ ] Lens clean
[ ] Vertical video mode (9:16)
[ ] Location services on (optional geo-tag)
[ ] Client consent line ready (below)

Client consent (say before filming)
-----------------------------------
"Hi, we're documenting our work for training and marketing — no names or flat numbers unless you're okay. Is that fine?"

If society/RWA: get committee approval for common-area shots only.

Phone settings
--------------
Video: 1080p minimum, 30fps, rear camera
Photo: HDR on, no flash unless dark basement
Stabilize: both hands or lean on wall — no shaky walk-and-talk
Audio (talking-head clips): face camera, reduce wind noise, re-record voiceover indoors if needed

20-minute shot list (check off on site)
--------------------------------------
A. Establishing (2 min)
[ ] Building exterior / society gate (no flat numbers in frame)
[ ] Team arriving with kit (hard hats if on site)

B. Problem / diagnosis (5 min)
[ ] Before: stain, crack, ponding water, failed paint peel
[ ] Close-up: leak path, pipe collar, expansion joint gap
[ ] Engineer pointing / mapping crack with chalk or marker
[ ] Wide: terrace with standing water OR damp ceiling below

C. Process (5 min)
[ ] Flood test water pour (15 sec clip)
[ ] Membrane roll / injection gun / UPV hammer in action
[ ] Detail: parapet upturn, drain clearance, joint sealant

D. After / proof (3 min)
[ ] Dry ceiling / dry terrace after work
[ ] Side-by-side before/after still (same angle)
[ ] Written diagnosis pack or warranty doc (blur client name)

E. Talking head (3 min) — founder or site engineer
[ ] 15–30 sec: "Here's what we found…" (one sentence hook)
[ ] 15–30 sec: myth-bust or checklist tip
[ ] Optional: client testimonial (with consent, no surname)

F. Stories extras (2 min)
[ ] Poll sticker prep: shoot 2 options (e.g. leak yes/no terrace)
[ ] "Send us your ceiling photo" CTA card (handwritten sign ok)

Minimum deliverables per shoot
------------------------------
3 vertical video clips (15–45 sec each)
5 still photos (before, after, detail, team, wide)
1 before/after pair (same framing)

File naming (before upload)
---------------------------
YYYY-MM-DD_location_clip1.mp4
Example: 2026-09-15_beltola_flood-test.mp4

Do NOT publish
--------------
Client flat numbers, Aadhaar, full phone numbers on documents
Unsafe work without PPE
Competitor logos or negative naming (say "single-coat contractor" not brand names)

After shoot (5 min at home)
---------------------------
[ ] Pick best 3 clips for week (see social-content-calendar.csv)
[ ] Trim to <60 sec for Reels
[ ] Add on-screen hook text in CapCut / Instagram editor (first 2 sec)
[ ] Queue captions from social-captions.txt (R## refs)
